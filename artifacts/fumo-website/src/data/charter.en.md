# Charter of the Federation of United Micronational Organisation (Version 7)
`CE5A0013`
Version VII
Constitutional Amendments Appoved in the 37th Regular Conference
## Chapter I puposes and Principles
`0918AA03C953A97B` `憲55E5E3DC`

### Article 1 
`CE9D2A6C197953A9` `憲20A19250`
1. The full Chinese name is "聯合微國家組織",abbreviation in Chinese as"聯微".
2. The English name of this organiztion is "Federation of United Micronations Organisation",abbreviation in English as 「FUMO」.
3. Unless otherwise stated or defined, the organization referred to in this Charter is a "Federation of United Micronational Organisation".

### Article 2
`DBDCA12DB7016B8C` `憲50EEB80E`
The purposes of this organization are to maintain international peace, eliminate threats to peace, prevent improper exclusion and discrimination, eliminate conduct that undermines international peace, and mediate disputes among micronations that may undermine a peaceful environment, thereby achieving international development, harmony, and stability.

### Article 3
`694342E758C54927` `憲882713FE`
The member states of this organization shall respect one another's sovereignty and fulfill the obligations set forth in this Charter to safeguard the rights and interests of all member states.

### Article 4
`2DC3003D8F054F8E` `憲0CEAE180`
This Charter constitutes the supreme governing instrument of this organization and shall have absolute authority. All personnel of this organization shall act in accordance with its provisions. All regulations of this organization and all normative resolutions adopted by its Deliberative Center must comply with this Charter. Matters not expressly regulated in detail by this Charter shall be governed by other organizational regulations.

### Article 5
`1FAB987C6C9525A1` `憲D7357760`
1. This organization shall establish the following principal organs：
    1. Deliberative Center
    2. Secretariat
    3. Council
2. This organization shall establish the following non-permanent organs:
    1. Boosting Committee
    2. Trusteeship Committee


### Article 6
`BB3F9517A65E0FC4` `憲AECE0B1A`
Unless otherwise specified by the regulations, resolutions of the Deliberative Center shall be adopted according to the following thresholds:

Ordinary Resolution Threshold: At least one-third of member states whose seats are not frozen must attend the meeting and vote. A resolution shall pass if the number of votes in favor exceeds the number of votes against.

Special Resolution Threshold: At least two-fifths of member states whose seats are not frozen must attend the meeting and vote. A resolution shall pass only if at least half of the votes cast are in favor.

## Chapter II Member and Observer States
`E5003CAE6B90A65E` `憲DFCE30E3`
### Article 7
`4ED845E57933508F` `憲4B66B979`
Countries joining the organization become participating States, and observer states and member state states are established.

### Article 8
`7F16A7DFA7E2BFA3` `憲5999FB10`
Member States must possess full sovereignty. If a Member States cease to possess full sovereignty, its status shall automatically be downgraded to that of an Observer State.

### Article 9
`5DEB5AE03F07106F` `憲480C36B0`
Participating States shall enjoy the rights and fulfill the obligations prescribed by this Charter and other applicable regulations of the Organization.

### Article 10
Countries seeking to join this organization must apply according to the relevant procedures to become observer states.
`C2CC8752F246C102` `憲4B10003C`

### Article 11
`6819717A646EB0B3` `憲11530D9D`
A state seeking promotion to Member State status must apply in accordance with the relevant procedures before it may become a Member State.

### Article 12
`D2266B045159ED5A` `憲BFE71EBF`
Where a Participating State faces circumstances that may affect its state seat, including a possible change of government, civil war, or division, it shall conduct internal consultations and subsequently publish the outcome or submit a proposal to the Deliberative Center for discussion.

## Chapter III The Deliberation Center
`2074BFB47C2A3A13` `憲B37D780E`

### Article 13
`34E96B39AB935841` `憲1CE8890C`
The Deliberative Center shall be the Organization's highest deliberative body.
It shall be responsible for establishing organizational laws and regulations and considering proposals.

### Article 14
`87721B326C42B09C` `憲9E1BD169`
1.The Council shall be responsible for the operation of the Delierative Center and shall appoint the Chair of the Deliberative Center.

2.The Deliberative Center shall have a Chair responsible for presiding over meetings.

3.The Deliberative Center shall have one or two Vice-Chairs, with the actual number determined by the Chair as appropriate. The Vice-Chairs shall assist the Chair and preside over meetings on the Chair's behalf when the Chair is unable to perform their duties.



### Article 15
`1511FE5FEFA856E7` `憲F455172B`
Member States shall have the rights to submit proposals, vote, and speak.

Observer States shall have the right to speak but shall not participate in voting or submit proposals, except for proposals seeking promotion to Member State status.

### Article 16
`C6250369B8E9AE5E` `憲CD66E3B9`
Unless otherwise provided by organizational regulations, the Deliberative Center shall conduct voting in accordance with the thresholds specified in Article 6 of this Charter.

### Article 17
`3F85265A9A49DD2F` `憲1977A568`
Proposals shall be voted on according to the principle of one vote per state, or by another voting method established by regulation or agreed upon by the Member States, provided that the voting method maintains equality among Member States.

Voting shall generally be conducted by recorded vote. Unless a proposal is subject to special provisions, it shall be decided according to the Ordinary Resolution Threshold.

### Article 18
`7D02C34254F221AA` `憲415F57EA`
All proposals shall be voted on at regular or extraordinary meetings.

Except for the election of the Secretary-General, admission proposals decided by the Deliberative Center, proposals to promote an Observer State to Member State status, proposals to downgrade a Member State or Observer State, proposals to remove a non-permanent Council member, and proposals to amend this Charter, representatives may submit ordinary proposals, which shall be decided according to the Ordinary Resolution Threshold.

### Article 19
`55F66E0E7928D1E4` `憲EF05773D`
Where there are excessive proposals, urgent circumstances, or other comparable situations, the Deliberative Center may convene an extraordinary meeting to vote on proposals, provided that at least five days remain before the next regular meeting and at least one-half of the Council members whose seats are not frozen have jointly endorsed the request.

### Article 20
`FBA58CD5425BFDC3` `憲9D0D28AC`
In accordance with the prescribed procedures, Member States may submit proposals to downgrade a Member State or remove an Observer State's seat.

### Article 21
`FAE162E1FFF3344F` `憲E84F7F8F`
Proposals concerning changes to state seats, including promotion or downgrading of state status, election or removal of Council members, and decisions concerning a state's right of representation, shall not be introduced or decided through the procedure for motions raised during a regular meeting.

### Article 22
`15B8BC94EABFB091` `憲7D1C71B7`
Detailed meeting procedures and related rules shall be prescribed by the Rules of Procedure of the Organization or other applicable regulations.

## Chapter IV The Secretariat

`AB197D2C140D3CDB` `憲23683F12`
### Article 23
`151A37F71C5569FE` `憲3AB6FE68`
The Secretariat shall be the Organization's highest administrative body. It shall have one Secretary-General, who shall serve as the Organization's chief administrative officer, direct the Organization's administrative affairs, represent the Organization externally, and possess the right to submit proposals to the Deliberative Center.

### Article 24
`278298BBAE7EBE3A` `憲EE16EB8B`
The Secretariat shall fulfill its duties in accordance with this Charter and the relevant regulations of the Organization.

### Article 25
`420B269C0F8A1AE1` `憲30586D53`
The Secretary-General shall serve a term of six months and may be re-elected for successive terms.

### Article 26
`BF44CCDA619ED965` `憲525FD2AD`
The Deliberative Center shall have the authority to elect and remove the Secretary-General. The procedures for election and removal shall be governed by the relevant regulations.

### Article 27
`2074BFB47C2A3A13` `憲B37D780E`
If the Secretary-General is unable to perform their duties, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant regulations.

## Chapter V The Council
`82144196781098E5` `憲47D62860`
### Article 28
`087516B1611017B6` `憲6BAEA015`
Council members shall fulfill their duties in accordance with the relevant regulations of the Organization.

### Article 29
`BE5F19D290A91EBF` `憲1F2EA8B0`
The number of Council seats and the procedures for electing Council members shall be governed by the relevant regulations of the Organization.

### Article 30
`0C418ACD099F657E` `憲2D035DDB`
The Council shall be responsible for and appoint the officers of the Deliberative Center.

### Article 31
`E91DF4274BB3DDB4` `憲4358AF0C`
Within seven days after a proposal submitted by a Member State has been officially announced and placed on the agenda for consideration by the Deliberative Center, two or more Council members may exercise their veto power.

If the veto threshold is reached, the proposal shall be removed from consideration. Any proposal previously approved by the Deliberative Center shall also immediately become invalid.
### Article 32
`4DD2F954DEBDFAC4` `憲07807670`
Veto power may be exercised only in relation to ordinary proposals and promotion proposals.

Veto power shall not be exercised in relation to the following proposals:

1.Amendments to this Charter;
2.Removal of Council members;
3.Admission to the Organization.

### Article 33
`2CB68203FFB2B498` `憲F91F26B0`
If a Council seat becomes vacant due to removal, withdrawal from the Organization, the dissolution of a state, or other circumstances, the vacancy shall be handled in accordance with the relevant regulations.

## Chapter VI Amendments and Supplementary Provisions to the Charter
`34ABDD814809E25F` `憲2939B71B`

### Article 34
`0CE386C095C97988` `憲16E3DE40`
Constitutional Amendments must be proposed by a member state or the Secretariat and require a Special Resolution Threshold.

### Article 35
`C1BCC1AB47BBB41E` `憲1B493200`
Any potential conflict of interest in the Charter will be interpreted by the Secretary-General.

### Article 36
`B75DF00CD290BC6B` `憲86EB275B`
Member States may not exercise their right to propose a motion based on facts that would render the following:

1. Any action that would clearly invalidate all or most of the provisions of the Charter of the Federation of United Micronational Organisation.
2. Any action that would seriously alter the organizational structure or disrupt the organizational order, rendering the organization unable to sustain its operations in a clearly predictable manner.
3. Any action that would clearly violate the provisions of the Charter of the Federation of United Micronational Organisation.

In the event of any violation of the foregoing provisions, the legislative center may refuse to accept the motion and shall notify the member state that made the motion of the reasons for the refusal in writing.

##### Article 8
> [!TIP] Original Text
>Member States must possess full sovereignty. They may not be autonomous regions or territories, principalities, counties, baronies, provinces, administrative districts, republics within another state (such as republics within Russia), special administrative regions, or other political entities lacking full sovereignty under the jurisdiction of another state. If a Member State loses its full sovereignty, it shall automatically be downgraded to Observer State status.

> [!Note]New Regulation
> FUMO **General Rules on Member State Sovereignty**

##### Article 9
Former Articles 9–11
> [!Tip]Original Text
> #### Article 9
>Member States of the Organization shall enjoy the following rights:
1.The right to submit motions and vote.
2.The right to elect and remove the Secretary-General and Council States of the Organization.
3.The right to participate in all activities of the Organization.
> #### Article 10
> Member States and Observer States of the Organization shall enjoy the following rights:
>1.The right to speak and participate in discussions at the General Assembly or regular meetings.
2.The right to appoint permanent representatives and special representatives, in accordance with organizational regulations, to attend or observe all meetings of the Organization.
3.The right to participate in certain activities of the Organization.
The right to use the Organization's resources in accordance with applicable rules.
> #### Article 11
> Member States and Observer States of the Organization shall have the following obligations:
> 1. To comply with and cooperate with this Charter, all organizational regulations, official duties, and resolutions.
> 2. To abide by the rulings or arbitration decisions of the Organization.
> 3. Not to interfere with the sovereignty of other Member States or Observer States.
> 4. To jointly safeguard the Organization's external image.
> 5. To actively participate in the affairs of the Organization.

> FUMO **Law on the Rights and Obligations of Participating State**

##### Article 10
Former Articles 12–13
> [!Tip]Original Text
> #### Articles 12
> A state seeking to join the Organization may appoint a representative to submit an application for membership to a Council State. Alternatively, the application may be forwarded to the Council by the Secretariat. Once approved by a Council State, the application shall take effect and the applicant shall obtain Observer State status.
> #### Articles 13
> A membership application submitted to a vote of the Deliberative Center shall pass if the number of votes in favor exceeds the number of votes against.
A state whose application is approved by the Deliberative Center shall obtain Observer State status after the conclusion of the regular meeting.
If the application is rejected, the state may not reapply to a Council State for an admission ruling until two regular meetings have passed. During this period, it may not request another membership vote by the Deliberative Center. 

> [!Note]New Regulation

##### Article 11
Former Articles 14
> [!Tip]Original Text
> 1. A Member State representative may submit a written motion to the Deliberative Center to upgrade a state's status. An Observer State seeking an upgrade must obtain the endorsement of at least one Member State. The Deliberative Center shall convene to consider the motion, which shall be subject to the special voting threshold.
> 2.Once an upgrade motion is approved, the state shall become a Member State after all agenda items of that regular meeting have concluded.
> 3. If an Observer State's upgrade motion is rejected by the Deliberative Center, another upgrade application concerning that Observer State may not be submitted by a Member State or an Observer State until two regular meetings have passed. During this period, the Deliberative Center shall not accept another upgrade motion concerning that Observer State unless a special resolution meeting the constitutional amendment threshold provides otherwise.

> [!Note]New Regulation
> FUMO **Rules on Upgrading to Member State Status**

##### Article 12
Former Articles 15-17
> [!Tip]Original Text
> #### Articles 15
> 1.Where the government of a Member State or Observer State merges with, is incorporated into, or transfers sovereignty to a new state, the relevant parties shall consult internally. If the former government transfers its full diplomatic autonomy to the resulting, incorporating, or successor state, that state shall decide, following internal consultation, whether to inherit or relinquish the former state's seat. If it elects to inherit the seat, it shall notify the Secretariat, after which the Deliberative Center shall decide whether to recognize the succession. Upon approval, the state shall continue to exercise the rights and assume the obligations associated with the seat. If approval is denied, the seat shall be forfeited.
> 2. Where the government of a Member State or Observer State merges with, is incorporated into, or transfers sovereignty to a new state, and the former government does not transfer its full diplomatic autonomy, the former government may retain the right to hold a seat in an international organization under its autonomy law, territorial agreement, or other relevant autonomy instrument. In accordance with Chapter II of this Charter, its status shall be downgraded directly to that of an Observer State. The state resulting from the merger, incorporation, or transfer must apply to join the Organization in accordance with this Charter.
> 3. If the states involved in a merger, incorporation, or transfer are all Member States of the Organization, they may, following internal consultation, choose either to retain one seat as a Member State while downgrading the other seats to Observer State status, or to downgrade all seats to Observer State status while granting Member State status to the state representing the parent state or central government.
> #### Articles 16
> Where a civil war or secession creates a dispute over the representation of a state seat held by a Member State or Observer State, the Deliberative Center may decide which regime is entitled to represent that seat.
> #### Articles 17
> Member States and Observer States may independently decide whether to withdraw from the Organization. A state withdrawing from the Organization shall submit a written declaration to the Secretariat. Withdrawal shall become official once confirmed by the Secretariat.
The parties shall update relevant information, such as official websites and micronational encyclopedia entries, within two months after the state's withdrawal.

> [!Note]New Regulation
> FUMO **Rules on Changes and Succession of Seats**

##### Articles 16
> [!Tip]Original Text
> Unless otherwise provided by organizational regulations, resolutions of the Deliberative Center shall be subject to the following voting thresholds:
> 1. Ordinary Threshold: At least one-third of Member States whose seats are not frozen must attend and vote. A resolution shall pass if votes in favor exceed votes against.
> 2. Special Threshold: At least two-fifths of Member States whose seats are not frozen must attend and vote. A resolution shall pass if at least half of the votes cast are in favor.

##### Articles 18
> [!Tip]Original Text
> All motions shall be voted on at regular or extraordinary meetings. Except for the election of the Secretary-General, membership admission resolutions of the Deliberative Center, motions to upgrade an Observer State seat to Member State status, motions to downgrade Member States or Observer States, motions to remove non-permanent Council States, and Charter amendments, representatives may submit ordinary motions.
An ordinary motion shall pass if votes in favor exceed votes against; otherwise, it shall fail.

##### Articles 20
> [!Tip]Original Text
> 1. A motion to downgrade a Member State or to remove or downgrade an Observer State may be submitted with written endorsements from three Member States. A motion to downgrade an Observer State shall be treated as equivalent to a motion to remove it from the Organization.
> 2. If a Member State or Observer State holds a seat belonging to an inactive state or a state that has effectively ceased to exist, a motion to downgrade that seat may be submitted without endorsements. The rules and detailed procedures for downgrading inactive state seats shall be prescribed by organizational regulations.

> [!Note]New Regulation

##### Articles 24
> [!Tip]Original Text
> The Secretariat shall have the following responsibilities:
> 1. Maintaining the Organization's deliberative operations and appointing the President and Vice-President of the General Assembly.
> 2. Managing the Organization's assets.
> 3. Organizing cultural activities.
> 4. Maintaining the Organization's security and order.
> 5. Fulfilling the requirements of this Charter and the Organization's regulations.
> 6. Implementing the resolutions of the Deliberative Center.

> [!Note]New Regulation
> FUMO **Law on the Obligations of the Secretariat**

##### Articles 26
Former Articles 31-32
> [!Tip]Original Text
> #### Articles 31
> A micronational individual may be nominated by one or more Member States to run for Secretary-General. Candidates may be citizens of any state within the Organization.
> 
> The election of the Secretary-General shall follow a two-round direct voting system. Member States shall vote in the Deliberative Center, and the candidate receiving the highest number of votes shall be elected Secretary-General. Detailed election procedures shall be prescribed by the relevant regulations governing the election of the Secretary-General.
> #### Articles 32
> 1. A motion to remove the Secretary-General must be jointly endorsed in writing by at least one-fifth of all Member States holding seats and submitted by a Member State to the Deliberative Center for a decision. The motion shall be subject to the special voting threshold.
> 2. If the removal motion fails, the same Secretary-General may not be subjected to another removal motion initiated by Member States during the same term of office.

> [!Note]Transfer Regulation
> FUMO **Rules on the Election of the Secretary-General** shall be consolidated into the **FUMO Rules on the Election and Removal of the Secretary-General**

##### Articles 27
> [!Tip]Original Text
> #### Articles 33
> If the Secretary-General is unable to perform their duties for any reason, they shall designate a member of the Secretariat who is a micronational individual to act on their behalf.
> #### Articles 34
> If the office of Secretary-General becomes vacant:
> 1. If more than two-thirds of the term remains, the Secretariat shall hold a by-election at the first regular organizational meeting following the vacancy. The newly elected Secretary-General shall serve until the end of the original term.
> 2. If one-third or less of the term remains, the Secretariat shall consult internally and nominate a member of the Secretariat to serve as Acting Secretary-General. The nominee shall assume the duties of the Secretary-General until the end of the original term after a confidence motion is approved at a Council States meeting.
> #### Articles 35
> If the Secretariat is largely unable to perform its duties, the Council States shall form an Administrative Council to exercise the Secretariat's functions. The Secretary-General or Acting Secretary-General shall be selected in accordance with the procedure provided in Article 16 of this Charter.

> [!Note]New Regulation
> FUMO **Rules on Incapacity and Vacancies in the Office of Secretary-General**

##### Articles 28
> [!Tip]Original Text
> Council States shall be Member States possessing a certain level of prestige and public credibility, and shall have the following responsibilities:
> 1. Mediating international disputes
> 2. Exercising veto powers
> 3. Assisting and guiding the operations of the Organization and the Secretariat
> 4. Promoting a positive public image of the Organization
> 5. Supporting the operations of the Deliberative Center

> [!Note]New Regulation
> FUMO **Law on the Obligations of Council States**

##### Articles 29
> [!Tip]Original Text
> The number of Council State seats shall be adjusted dynamically according to the total number of Member States. As a general rule, one Council State seat shall be allocated for every eight Member States.
> 
> The number of Council State seats shall be no fewer than three and no more than thirteen, and the total shall always be an odd number. If the calculation produces an even number, the nearest greater odd number shall be used.
> 
> Council States shall be selected from among the Member States by a resolution of the Deliberative Center. Their term of office shall be six months, and they may be re-elected. They may also be removed in accordance with the procedures set out in this Charter. Detailed election and removal procedures shall be prescribed by the relevant regulations.

> [!Note]Transfer Regulation
> FUMO **Rules on the Election of Council States** shall be consolidated into the FUMO **Rules on the Election and Removal of Council States**

##### Articles 30
> [!Tip]Original Text
> 1. The Council States shall be responsible for the operation of the Deliberative Center and shall appoint its President
> 2. The Deliberative Center shall have a President, who shall preside over its meetings
> 3. The Deliberative Center shall have one or two Vice-Presidents, with the actual number determined by the President as circumstances require. The Vice-Presidents shall assist the President in presiding over meetings and shall act on the President's behalf when the President is unable to perform their duties

> [!Note]New Regulation

Former Articles 41-43

> 1. If more than two-thirds of the term remains, the Secretariat shall hold a by-election at the first regular organizational meeting following the vacancy. The newly elected Council State shall serve until the end of the original term.
> 2. If one-third or less of the term remains, the next eligible state shall be appointed according to the vote ranking from that Council State election. It shall serve until the end of the original term.
